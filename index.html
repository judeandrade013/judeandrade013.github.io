Selected work

2020–2026 · 5 case studies

  1. 01Taxi and for-hire transportationSpring Valley’s taxi law could see the driver, but not the business behind the rideComplaints about unlicensed cabs pointed toward tougher enforcement. Talking with every local cab company and reading the village code suggested the law’s definitions were the weak point, and that new penalties would mostly land on small, licensed drivers.Led the research and drafted the definitions · Local laws adopted by the Village Board · Enforcement effects not yet measuredRead case study →
  2. 02KOALA, product researchWhy timeshare owners gave up on the first page of a listingThe listing flow asked for a reservation number many owners never receive, and it lost their progress if they went back a step. Interviews with owners showed where the flow broke down. Letting owners save progress and making some fields optional raised the success rate on that page.First dedicated UX researcher, 2020–2022 · First-page success rate 33% → 46%Read case study →
  3. 03Municipal website and facility requestsWhat happens after a resident submits a form?Booking a village park or community center used to take two to four trips to Village Hall. I mapped the request from both the resident’s side and the Clerk’s, then set up and tested online requests for seven facilities within the limits of the village’s CMS.Led the research and the build · Forms Index and online requests live · Online payment awaiting approvalRead case study →
  4. 04Memorial Park floodingThe park floods every few years. What would it take to qualify for state funding?A new engineering study looked like a prerequisite for a state grant, at a time when the Village was running a deficit. The County’s own flood model showed the causes were downstream of the park, and written guidance from the state DEC showed that an existing regional report could qualify a narrower project.Led the investigation and funding strategy · Grant eligibility confirmed · No award or construction yetRead case study →
  5. 05Master fee scheduleWhat does it actually cost the Village to process a land-use application?Fees didn’t track the work: departments estimated a minor site plan at about three days of staff time and a major one at more than a week. I inventoried all 172 fees, traced the roughly 45 that have an application, proposed tiers by size and complexity, and separated outside review costs into applicant escrow.Led the analysis and proposal · Adopted by the Village Board · Revenue figures are projectionsRead case study →
