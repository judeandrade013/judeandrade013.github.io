A quick heuristic review of go-koala.com

A 14-page review of KOALA’s website organized around ten psychology principles (thinking, limitations, mistakes, memory, social, attention, information, processing, mental models, and the visual system), adapted from Susan Weinschenk’s work on how people use screens. It covers destination-page readability, the booking form’s layout and labels, and validation and error handling, and it ends with what to test next.

It is a review, not a usability test. It identifies likely friction and recommends where to look harder; it does not measure outcomes. KOALA acted on some of its recommendations.
